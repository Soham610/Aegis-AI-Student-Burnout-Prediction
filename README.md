# Aegis — AI System for Predicting Student Burnout & Dropout Risk

## Overview

Aegis is an AI-powered predictive analytics platform designed to identify students who may be at risk of academic burnout, elevated stress levels, and potential dropout. The system leverages machine learning algorithms to analyze academic and behavioral indicators, enabling educational institutions to take proactive measures and support students before issues become critical.

This project was developed during a hackathon to explore the use of Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning in educational analytics.

---

## Problem Statement

Student burnout and academic dropout have become increasingly common due to factors such as:

- Academic pressure
- Mental health challenges
- Reduced engagement
- Poor academic performance
- Lack of timely intervention

Educational institutions often struggle to identify at-risk students early enough to provide effective support.

---

## Solution

Aegis uses machine learning models trained on student-related data to:

- Predict burnout risk
- Estimate dropout probability
- Analyze academic stress levels
- Identify performance-related concerns
- Generate actionable insights for educators

The goal is to provide an early-warning system that enables institutions to make data-driven decisions and improve student outcomes.
